## Talking points (in this order)
1. HOOK (first words, one sentence): Fifteen minutes a day, no app: here's the routine.
2. Do it once a day. Three new words is plenty.
3. Minutes zero to five: anchor. Write one sentence about your own life with each new word.
4. Minutes five to ten: recall. Cover the stronger word. Read "very angry" and say "livid" before you look.
5. Minutes ten to fifteen: speak. Say two new sentences with each word, out loud, as if you were telling a friend or a colleague.
6. YOUR TURN (an instruction, then a clear stop, then the example): Your turn. Say one sentence about your week with "swamped", out loud. (stop, silence) For example: "I was swamped at work this week."
7. No streaks to protect, no decks to manage. Just keep going.
8. Five ways to say never give up. The scene comes first and the word comes last:
   - A runner in the rain, still running: keep going.
   - A girl falls off her bike and gets straight back on: determined.
   - A little dog that won't let go of its rope toy: tenacious.
   - Waves hitting the rocks, again and again: relentless.
   - A donkey that won't move, whatever you do: obstinate. That's the bad kind: just stubborn.
9. THE ACTION FOR TOMORROW: Tomorrow, pick three new words and set a timer for fifteen minutes.
10. CTA: Find more ways to say "never give up" on anotherwordfor.net. (Say the website as "another word for dot net".)
11. TEASER (last words): Next: why your mind goes blank.

## Delivery notes
- Point 1 is the first sound of the recording. Say it as a clear, confident statement.
- Points 3, 4 and 5 each start with the block: "Minutes zero to five", "Minutes five to ten", "Minutes ten to fifteen". Say the block, a short stop, then its name (anchor, recall, speak), then the step.
- Point 4: say "very angry", a tiny stop, then "livid", stressed a little.
- Point 6 is the learner's turn. Say the instruction, then a clear stop so the learner can speak, then the example as its own new sentence, calm and clear. The example never comes before or inside the instruction.
- Point 8: keep the five in this order: keep going, determined, tenacious, relentless, obstinate. Scene first, word last, and stress the word a little. "That's the bad kind: just stubborn" is said a little lighter, with a smile.
- "Tenacious" is "tuh-NAY-shus". "Relentless" is "ri-LENT-less". "Obstinate" is "OB-stih-nut". "Livid" is "LIV-id".
- About one minute in total. If you must shorten something, shorten the pauses between points, never drop a point.
