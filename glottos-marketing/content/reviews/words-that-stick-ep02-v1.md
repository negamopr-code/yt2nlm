Reviewed 2026-09-30 by glottos-viewer-critic (headless autopilot step "episode 2: critic").
File: `state/shorts/words-that-stick-ep02-v1/final.mp4` (53.30 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, AAC). Unit `shorts/units/ep02_v1.json`.
I only reviewed. Nothing was rendered, changed or uploaded.

## Verdict: PARTLY

The video gives the right reason (you recognise words but can't recall them) and three self-tests that really work. But the one action it teaches, covering the answer, is **never said out loud**. The "what to do tomorrow" line is a single sentence in the middle of the video with no "cover" and no "tomorrow". The last thing the viewer hears is an abstract line about memory, not the action.

**Ship-blockers: none found.** No spelled-out words, no watermark, no answer spoiled before its pause, no false number. Everything below is "fix before publish" or "nice-to-have".

## 1. Pain (learners' own words) and what "solved" means
Verbatim quotes from the PROBLEM 01 synthesis (NotebookLM source 60678678, section 3):
- "I can understand 100% of the video, but when I try to speak, my mind goes blank"
- "I mostly avoid communicating in a crowd because I dont remember the words."
- "confidence on talking I forgetting everything as soon as I start talking"
- "when I try to speak I forgot every sentence in my mind !! My mind became blank ..."
- "I can write paragraphs in English without any difficulty but as soon as i speak my mind just shuts down"
- "I understand everything... But at the end of the day, if i try to open my mouth for speak english, its blank."
- "my brain just went into a wall of white noise, i could access nothing, formulate nothing"

**The pain in one sentence (learner's view):** "I know these words when I see them, but when I have to speak they are gone."
**Solved means:** tomorrow I stop reading my list again. I cover the English word, look at the meaning, force myself to say the word, and only then check. I know that this struggle is the part that works.

## 4. Takeaway test
One action a viewer can do: **"Cover the answer column of my list, say the word from the meaning, then check."** It can be named, but only by combining panel 4's picture with the 35.5 s sentence. A listen-only viewer (most Shorts viewers half-watch) gets "read the meaning, say the word out loud, check the answer" without knowing what to hide. So the verdict stays at PARTLY.

## 5. Retention risks
- Length (53 s) is fine for the content. The three quizzes are the retention engine and they work.
- Dead canvas: every beat uses only the top ~45% of the 1920 px frame. Panels are about 1080x420 and the lower half is empty cream. Pictures and bar labels are smaller than they need to be.
- Reveals are 0.8 s each (crucial, swamped, urgent). That is the moment of reward in a quiz, and it flashes by. The swamped example sentence cannot be read.
- 8.8 s static quiz card at 20.4-29.2 while the voice is off-topic.
- Pauses are digital zero (-270 dB at 27.75-29.10), not room tone. On headphones this can sound like the audio dropped out rather than "your turn". Nice-to-have.
- Video bitrate is 426 kb/s, so there is visible blocking in near-white areas (panel 3 background, panel 4 top). YouTube re-encodes anyway. Nice-to-have: a lower CRF.

## 6. Trust risks
- Facts: 61% vs 40%, one week, same study time is correct for Roediger & Karpicke 2006 (Exp. 2, prose passages). The voice says "of what they studied", not "% of words". OK. Put the source line in the description, and say it was about texts, not word lists.
- Overclaim: "rereading ... **really only** trains your brain to recognize" is stronger than the article's "mostly". Minor.
- The voice's "link them to clear images" during a recall quiz sends a mixed message (picture method vs recall). It is not false, but it blurs the one lesson.
- English: "are exactly what make the memory stronger" is acceptable, though "what makes" is more usual. Everything else heard sounds natural.
- Patch leftovers:
  - **Panel 1 smoke (0.0-5.56 s, the hook frame): visible rectangular mosaic blocks** from the downscale-upscale patch in both plumes. At full resolution it looks like something was censored. No letters are legible any more. Fix before publish (not blocking: at phone thumbnail size it reads as smoke).

  - Panel 4 painted-over header: clean in the source panel (contrast-boosted check). There is only faint compression noise in the video.
  - Watermark: none on any frame. The logo is the channel avatar.
