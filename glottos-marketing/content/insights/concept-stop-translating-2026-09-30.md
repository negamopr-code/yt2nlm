## Marcus read
- The demand is huge and evergreen. The pain is "I understand but can't speak / I translate in my head".
- The packaging is the lever: a pain-flip title plus a practice promise plus a level tag.
- The format is cheap. We already have Kokoro + pictures + builder.
- Red flag: the channel's typical video gets about 3k views; two packaged hits carry it. Copy the packaging, not the channel.
- It is off the synonym lane. Bridge: a shadowing / "repeat after me" section built from our better-word sentences.
