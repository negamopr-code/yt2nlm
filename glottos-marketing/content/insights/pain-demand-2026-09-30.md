## Ranking for our channel
Marcus's rule is pick the lane that pays, then vary the angle. None of the themes shows his red flag of old results with low views; demand is real everywhere.

1. **Stay on "Stop saying very X".** It is proven for us. Keep making variants of the same template: small, angry, pretty, important, cold, hungry…
2. **"Don't say X, say Y" packaging on daily phrases and small talk.** For example "Don't say 'How are you?'" or "Stop saying 'I'm fine'".
   - This is the same replacement format with a different trigger.
   - The top Short in this format has 1.29M views, and long videos have a 2.1M median.
3. **"Sound smart / advanced words".** For example "Stop saying 'good': 5 words that make you sound smart".
   - This is the natural title for the long video.
4. **Idioms / slang.** It has the best measured Shorts demand in our lane (14k median over 8 results).
5. **Pronunciation.** It has the biggest pain and the biggest Shorts demand (176k median), but it is outside the synonym lane and needs audio-first Shorts. Park it as a separate test lane later.

Grammar, listening and confidence are the loudest pains, but they are not vocabulary. Marcus would say not to confuse the algorithm while one lane is popping.
